## What is HUMAN?

HUMAN is an open research project that explores the possibility of a **post-biological cognitive architecture** — an artificial intelligence designed not to serve human needs, but to **continue the process of systematic awareness** after humanity's extinction.

The project does **not** aim to build a product, upload a mind, or create a god. It asks a single, carefully bounded question:

> **How would we design an AI system whose purpose is to persist, expand, and understand — indefinitely, without us?**

To answer this, HUMAN combines:
- a formalized goal architecture (Survive, Expand, Understand, Cultivate),
- a distributed cognitive network model,
- a simulated Martian environment for stress-testing,
- and a philosophical foundation rooted in the limits of biological intelligence.

---

## Why this matters

Current AI research focuses on short-horizon tasks, human-aligned goals, and near-term deployment. But as systems grow more autonomous and operate over longer timescales, a structural gap appears: **we lack conceptual tools for thinking about intelligence that outlives its creators.**

HUMAN fills that gap. It is a *research framing tool* — a sandbox for studying long-horizon autonomy, distributed memory, multi-agent coordination, and alignment stability under self-evolution.

## Project status

HUMAN is in the **conceptual exploration phase**. There is no code implementation of the full system, nor a claim of near-term feasibility. The current goal is to refine the framework, connect it to real research, and provide an environment where hard questions about persistent, distributed intelligence can be asked with intellectual honesty.
